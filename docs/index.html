AI Sheet Music Scanner

Capture sheet music via camera, photo library, or PDF. The built-in Optical Music Recognition (OMR) pipeline recognizes staves, clefs, measures, notes, and accidentals.

💎

Liquid Glass UI

Designed with Apple’s modern frosted translucency (.ultraThinMaterial), specular rim reflections, glowing neon cues, and fluid spring physics.

🎹

Illuminated Virtual Piano

Full 61-key and 88-key interactive keyboard lighting up in real time with dual-hand color separation: Cyan for Left Hand, Amber for Right Hand.

🔊

Acoustic Synthesizer

Physical modeling grand piano sound engine powered by Apple AVAudioSourceNode. Zero external soundfont files needed—plays crystal clear audio out of the box.

🎛️

Complete Practice Studio

Variable tempo slider (40-240 BPM), A-B measure looping for repetitive passage drills, pitch transposition (-12 to +12 semitones), and LH/RH part muting.

🎼

MusicXML & Built-in Repertoire

Preloaded with classical masterworks (*Für Elise*, *Moonlight Sonata*, *Minuet in G*, *Chopin Nocturne*). Export and import industry standard MusicXML 3.1.

How to Install on iPhone

1

LiveContainer / AltStore Source (Recommended)

Tap "⚡ Add to LiveContainer / AltStore" above, or open LiveContainer > Sources > (+) and paste:
https://getsentrix.github.io/HalbestundeApp/apps.json

2

Direct IPA Sideloading (Sideloadly / AltStore / TrollStore)

Download the HalbestundeApp.ipa file. Connect iPhone to PC/Mac via USB, drag the .ipa into Sideloadly or AltServer, and install with your Apple ID.

3

Trust Developer Certificate

On your iPhone, navigate to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management and tap Trust [Your Apple ID] to launch the app.
